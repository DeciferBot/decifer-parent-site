# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two buyers now, deliberately, as of the August 2026 positioning revision. Neither has yet been dropped in favour of the other; that call is still open (see below).

1. Owners, operations directors and marketing leads of businesses with roughly 10 to 200 people, mostly in Dubai and the wider Gulf, plus the UK and Singapore. They arrive on a laptop in an office, often from a forwarded link. They have seen AI demos and been burned by pilots that never shipped. Their job on this site: decide in under a minute whether Decifer is a real company worth a 30-minute call.
2. CTOs and CMOs evaluating an AI implementation partner for a larger organisation, and investors assessing whether the method is repeatable. They are less worried about legitimacy than about governance: where exactly does AI touch the output, who owns the boundary, and does it hold under audit. They forward the `/capabilities` page internally before they forward anything else.

A third, smaller audience: people who want one of the three public products (Decifer Markets, Decifer Learning, Decifer Marketing). They need a clear path off this site to the product.

**Open decision, not yet made:** whether the SME funnel (the fixed-fee two-week audit, the founder-replies-personally trust move) stays as the entry motion for both audiences, or whether serving enterprise buyers eventually needs its own commercial shape. Every case shape on file is still SME-scale; the site says so explicitly on `/capabilities` rather than implying otherwise.

## Product Purpose

decifer.io is the company site for Decifer, an AI implementation company. Its purpose: put a working AI system inside a business, not a slide deck about one. Most of a job stays deterministic; AI earns its place only at the point that needs judgement, inside a boundary written down in advance. decifer.io sells four services (AI agents, data and reporting automation, AI product builds, a fixed-fee two-week AI audit), presents that discipline as a capability matrix (`/capabilities`) rather than a list of features, and shows three public products as proof the method runs unattended before it is ever sold to a client. Success is a qualified enquiry: a named person books a call or emails with a real problem.

## Brand Personality

Plain, exacting, accountable.

Voice: a senior engineer explaining to a business owner what will be built, what it will not do, and how they will be able to check. Short sentences. Specific nouns. Numbers only when they come from `src/app/data/proof.ts`. The company says "we" and the founder says "I". Never "empower", "transform", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "world-class". No em dashes.

The emotional goal is relief: "these people will tell me the truth and they will actually ship."

## Anti-references

- The 2024 AI agency template: dark navy, dot grid, orange or purple glow, centered hero, glass cards, a tiny uppercase label above every section, "01 / 02 / 03" markers, stock robot imagery.
- Manifesto sites: thirteen sections of doctrine before a single proof.
- Enterprise consultancies: stock photos of handshakes, "solutions" with no deliverables, no prices, no faces.
- Crypto-style hype: big gradient numbers, rocket language, "revolutionary".

## Design Principles

1. Proof before pitch. Real screenshots of real products, real figures with a source, a real founder, before any claim.
2. Say what you will not do. Every service lists who it is not for. Every case lists what was deliberately not automated. This is the differentiator; design must give it room.
3. The client owns it. Tools are shown as things the client will hold, not as partner badges we collect.
4. One idea per section. Eight sections on the home page, each with one job, each scannable in three seconds.
5. The parent of a family. Three products have three visual worlds. This site is the calm, neutral parent that makes them look related.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text 4.5:1 minimum, including on the orange sections. Every interactive element keyboard reachable with a visible focus ring. Reduced motion gets crossfades only. No information carried by colour alone. Plain English throughout because many readers have English as a second language.
