# What this site is for

decifer.io is a lead generation asset, not a research artifact, a portfolio
or a reference library. It has exactly two jobs:

1. **Pull traffic in.** Search, shares and forwarded links.
2. **Convert that traffic into a qualified enquiry.** A named person with a
   real problem, in the inbox.

**A page that does not do one of those two things is failing at its job.**
That applies to every page, every article, every tool, every section within
them. There is no "just informational" surface here and no charity.

## What this means when you build or edit anything

- **Every interaction ends somewhere.** A verdict, a score, a calculation, an
  article, a case study: the reader must be handed a next step that is theirs,
  not a generic footer. A dead end is a lost lead, however good the content is.
- **Carry the intent forward.** If a reader has already told the site
  something (numbers in a tool, the process they care about, the article they
  read), carry it into the next screen instead of asking again. Every field
  they have to retype costs conversions.
- **Route by what they told you.** A negative verdict converts differently
  from a positive one. Send each outcome somewhere that fits it: a call, the
  fixed-fee assessment, a smaller first build, a service page.
- **Traffic side counts too.** Titles, descriptions, headings, structured data
  and internal links are part of the job. A page nobody can find fails the
  first test whatever it says.

## What this does not mean

Honesty is the conversion mechanism here, not an obstacle to it. The site
says what it will not do, tells a reader when a build is not worth it, and
shows the arithmetic. Keep all of that: it is why the enquiries that arrive
are qualified. What is never acceptable is the honest answer delivered as a
dead end. Say the true thing, then say what to do about it, then give them
the way to do it.
