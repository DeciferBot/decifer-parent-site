Dubai is moving its private sector to agentic AI. Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed, Crown Prince of Dubai, [launched the programme in May 2026](https://www.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/may/04-05/hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-dubai-private-sector-shift-to-agentic-ai-within-two-years), and in June the Higher Committee for Future Technology Development and the Digital Economy, which he chairs, reviewed the executive plan. The targets: reach 295,000 companies within two years, develop and deliver 100 specialised AI assistants, and support the establishment of 50 agentic AI companies, with delivery running through Dubai Chamber initiatives including training tracks, incubators and dedicated funds.

That is what was announced. This article separates what is known from what is not, and says what a business in Dubai should actually do about it, because we are already seeing the announcement used as a sales tool, and not always honestly.

## What was actually announced

Three numbers, from the official announcements as reported by the state news agency and the local press: 295,000 companies to be empowered with agentic AI over two years, 100 specialised AI assistants to be developed and delivered, and 50 agentic AI companies to be supported into existence. "Agentic AI" here means systems that can carry out tasks and manage steps of a process with a degree of autonomy, rather than chatbots that only answer questions.

The framing throughout is support: training tracks, incubators, funding channels, and ready-made AI assistants for common business functions. Coverage is easy to find from [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/business/technology/dubai-to-support-295000-companies-with-agentic-ai-under-new-sheikh-hamdan-backed-plan) and [Fast Company Middle East](https://fastcompanyme.com/news/dubai-wants-295000-companies-to-embrace-the-next-wave-of-ai/), among others.

## What is not known yet

As we write this, there is no public application form, no published eligibility list, and no detailed timetable for which sectors get which assistants first. The developments since June: Dubai Chambers formed an Executive Committee for Agentic AI to approve the training tracks and design the incubators, and in August 2026 it [signed an agreement with India's Nasscom](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/2026/08/19/dubai-accelerates-india-deal-to-adopt-agentic-ai-in-private-sector/) to help grow agentic AI businesses in Dubai. Neither includes an application process. If a vendor tells you they can register you for the programme today, ask them for the official link. The honest channels to watch are Dubai Chamber's announcements and the Dubai government's official communications, and we will update this article as the details land.

## What the programme is not

It is not a deadline, and it is not a compliance requirement. There is no fine for not adopting AI by a date. We wrote about this in [seven AI myths Dubai business owners keep paying for](/blog/seven-ai-myths-dubai-business-owners-keep-paying-for): urgency converts, so some sellers frame the plan as a countdown. Buying the wrong system quickly is worse than buying the right one calmly, and the programme's own shape, training and support over two years, says calm is the intended pace.

The businesses that will get the most from the programme are the ones that arrive knowing what they need. That preparation is free and takes an afternoon:
