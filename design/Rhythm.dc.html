Same content. Somewhere to look.

The home page is 9,400px tall and eleven of its thirteen sections are the same white panel with two columns of text in it. Nothing is wrong with any one section; the problem is that they arrive in one unbroken texture, so a reader with forty seconds finds no foothold.

What actually does the work

Use the screenshots

Three 1440×900 captures of the live products already sit in public/products/ and appear nowhere on the home page. They are the only real imagery the site owns.

Give every row a mark

One icon set, one hue per industry and per service. A reader recognises the row before reading it, and the same colour follows that thing to every other page.

Make the numbers big

Fifteen verified figures are currently set at body size inside paragraphs. A figure tile with its source underneath is proof that also happens to break the page up.

Change the ground

Five bands instead of one, alternating. Costs nothing in copy, and it is the single change that turns a report back into a page.
